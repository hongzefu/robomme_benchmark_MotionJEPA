- topic_key: unmask_swap
- summary: Inner partner rule, answering the user's "最近2邻？": the inner swap partner is the single nearest other inner bin, measured by Euclidean distance on 2 axes (x,y). It is not "the 2 nearest neighbours". The only "2 nearest" code is the helper pair _compute_dynamic_swap_candidates / _select_swap_pair_from_positions, and nothing calls it (dead code in VideoUnmaskSwap, ButtonUnmaskSwap and VideoRepick). Initiators are a fixed a,b,c,a,b,c rotation taken from two main-stream draws. Each partner is resolved lazily at the first step of its window from actual poses. Hidden cubes do not travel with their bins: they are teleported away for the whole swap span and re-placed under their bin's final XY at the last swap end. A pure numpy/torch replica of the sampling was checked value-for-value against all 20 frozen V4 spec rows (20/20 exact). The same replica reproduces all 8 observed runtime BinCollisionErrors (1 in the rollout, 7 in V6). All 8 are an inner swap path hitting a third, static inner bin; none involves a distractor. Over 300 synthetic layouts per env, 1.3% of VUS and 10.7% of BUS episodes have such an inner-vs-inner collision that V4 never checks at reset. Making the outer bins swap by copying the rule literally (3 distractors in the V4 ring, nearest-neighbour partner, same two-lane path) mostly fails. The median path length is 0.46-0.49 m, so paths cross the inner area. Only 31% (VUS) and 29% (BUS) of episodes are collision-free and stay in camera view, and in BUS 38% of outer swap windows (787/2084) pass within the button keep-out circle. The recommended V5 design mirrors the inner rule exactly (same window, nearest-neighbour partner among distractors, the same swap_flat_two_lane path, cubes follow their bins). It is planned at reset on the existing dedicated distractor RNG stream with one appended randperm. If a window's first choice fails, the planner deterministically tries the next initiator in the permutation, and it redraws the whole distractor layout only as a last resort. Every window is certified by a joint 2-pair continuous-sweep proof, which reuses the repo's _prove_pair unchanged (scratch generalisation matches check_swap_sweep 15/15). With 6 distractors in the V4 ring, the first layout attempt is fully feasible for 99.0% (VUS) and 96.7% (BUS) of episodes under the strict criterion; with 10 distractors both reach 100%. The design adds zero control steps. Without a cheap certified pre-filter, it roughly doubles the per-window collision-check wall-clock (0.8-1.8 s vs 0.4-0.9 s now); with the pre-filter it drops to 3-55 ms. easy/medium/hard are untouched, and the xhard main RNG stream is unchanged.

  [0]
    - question: 现在内部swap的机制是什么？
    - answer: 1. Count and timing. The swap count is drawn once per episode: VUS xhard [8,12] from native configs.xhard, BUS xhard [6,8] from decision.swap_count_range.xhard. Swaps run back-to-back in windows [64+33k, 64+33(k+1)]; 33 = round(50/1.5).
2. Initiators. Swap k's initiator is fixed in advance by a cyclic rotation a,b,c,a,b,c,... of 3 initiators drawn once from the main stream (swap_initiator_indices + swap_initiator_third).
3. Partner. At the first step of window k, the code picks the nearest other inner bin in XY from the actual current positions (strict <, ties to the lower spawn index).
4. Motion. Both bins move along swap_flat_two_lane: straight chord plus opposite ±0.07 m sinusoidal lane offsets, smoothstep timing, blended yaw, exact swapped poses at the window end. The other inner bins are pinned.
5. Hidden cubes. They are teleported off-table during [64, last_end) and put back under their own bin at last_end.
6. Resulting structure. Because partners are mutual nearest neighbours, 60.7% of VUS and 81.9% of BUS episodes only ever use 2 disjoint pairs. 23.9% / 26.7% of swaps immediately undo the previous one, and 64% (VUS) / 84% (BUS) of hidden cubes visit only 2 positions.
    - evidence: Code: VideoUnmaskSwap.py/ButtonUnmaskSwap.py :: __init__, _load_scene, _refresh_swap_schedule, step; statechange.py :: swap_flat_two_lane, lift_and_drop_objectA_onto_objectB. Replica verified 20/20 against specs.jsonl (m0_verify_replica.py). ## blind_spots
  * Only one simulator reset (VUS seed 4500300) was run. No V5 code exists, so the effect of outer swaps on demo success (especially BUS, where the robot presses buttons during swaps) is not measured.
  * The robot arm and button geometry are not in the collision model. Button risk is approximated by centre-distance thresholds (0.122 m keep-out, 0.0955 m possible base contact).
  * Camera visibility uses the conservative B13 fitted formula (visible_on_camera), not rendering, and ignores occlusion by the arm.
  * Synthetic inner layouts use a replica whose spawn_random_bin obstacle OBB is approximated (yawed 0.03 half-square padded by min_gap). It is exact on all 20 spec rows but was not cross-checked with get_actor_obb elsewhere.
  * M2/M2b use nominal positions and exact swapped end states. Real start states deviate up to about 0.7 mm (unfinished previous swap) and z differs by 1.3 mm before swaps; the effect on certification margins is not quantified.
  * The 'tight ring' is my own assumption (inner rectangle = centre extent + 0.03 + 0.03 + 0.04, width 0.10). The sibling topic may define it differently.
  * The sibling topic's final distractor count and ring are unknown. Numbers are given for 3/6/10/15/20 distractors in the V4, padded and tight rings.
  * Alternative trajectories (asymmetric lane offsets, arcs that follow the ring) were not simulated.
  * The layout bias from fallback and redraws is characterised only by side distribution and redraw rates, not by any downstream difficulty metric.
  * Machine load was 35-95 during timing, so absolute wall-clock numbers are inflated; the ratios are the meaningful part.
